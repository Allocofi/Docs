SURE uses a multi-layer risk framework covering its **foreign-exchange statistical arbitrage** Fund, statistical models, leverage, liquidity, counterparties, Token operations and product tracking.

Risk controls are designed to identify and reduce risk. They cannot eliminate losses, guarantee a stable NAV or ensure uninterrupted distributions and redemptions.

## Risk Measurement Framework

Risk is assessed at the SURE product level, the underlying Fund level and across the Fund's approved FX trading accounts.

| Risk Dimension | Metric | What It Measures |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Token tracking | Tracking Difference | Difference between SURE total return and underlying Fund total return |
| Portfolio directionality | Net FX Exposure Ratio | Residual directional currency exposure relative to Fund NAV |
| Total position size | Gross Leverage Ratio | Aggregate long and short FX exposure before offsetting |
| Currency concentration | Single Currency Exposure Ratio | Exposure attributable to one currency relative to Fund NAV |
| Strategy concentration | Pair Concentration Ratio | Share of gross exposure attributable to one statistical relationship |
| Portfolio loss | Maximum Drawdown | Largest decline from a historical peak NAV to a subsequent trough |
| Distribution coverage | Distribution Coverage Ratio | Net return available relative to the fixed distribution paid |
| Liquidity | Estimated Exit Time | Time and expected cost required to reduce or close positions |

### Tracking Difference

Tracking difference measures whether SURE has performed differently from the underlying Fund after distributions and product-level costs:

$$
\mathrm{Tracking\ Difference}
=
\mathrm{SURE\ Total\ Return}
-
\mathrm{Underlying\ Fund\ Total\ Return}
$$

Tracking difference may result from cash balances, conversion costs, fees, distribution timing, settlement delays, rounding and valuation corrections.

### Net FX Exposure Ratio

The Net FX Exposure Ratio measures residual directional currency exposure after long and short positions are offset:

$$
\mathrm{Net\ FX\ Exposure\ Ratio}
=
\frac{
\left|\mathrm{Net\ Delta\ Adjusted\ FX\ Notional}\right|
}{
\mathrm{Fund\ NAV}
}
\times 100\%
$$

A lower ratio generally indicates that offsetting FX positions are more closely matched. A ratio of zero does not mean that the portfolio is risk-free.

### Gross Leverage Ratio

Gross leverage measures total FX exposure before long and short positions are offset:

$$
\mathrm{Gross\ FX\ Notional}
=
\sum_{j=1}^{n}
\left|
\mathrm{Delta\ Adjusted\ Notional}_j
\right|
$$

$$
\mathrm{Gross\ Leverage\ Ratio}
=
\frac{
\mathrm{Gross\ FX\ Notional}
}{
\mathrm{Fund\ NAV}
}
\times 100\%
$$

<Warning>
  A portfolio may have low net directional exposure while maintaining substantial gross leverage. Statistical neutrality should therefore be assessed together with leverage, margin, liquidity, correlation and counterparty exposure.
</Warning>

### Single Currency Exposure Ratio

This ratio measures residual exposure to one currency across all positions:

$$
\mathrm{SCER}_i
=
\frac{
\left|
\mathrm{Net\ Exposure\ to\ Currency}\ i
\right|
}{
\mathrm{Fund\ NAV}
}
\times 100\%
$$

### Pair Concentration Ratio

The Pair Concentration Ratio measures how much of the portfolio's gross exposure is allocated to one statistical relationship:

$$
\mathrm{PCR}_i
=
\frac{
\mathrm{Gross\ Notional\ of\ Pair\ Strategy}\ i
}{
\mathrm{Total\ Gross\ FX\ Notional}
}
\times 100\%
$$

### Maximum Drawdown

Maximum Drawdown measures the largest percentage decline from a historical peak NAV to the lowest subsequent NAV before a new peak is reached:

$$
\mathrm{MDD}
=
\frac{
\mathrm{Peak\ NAV}
-
\mathrm{Subsequent\ Trough\ NAV}
}{
\mathrm{Peak\ NAV}
}
\times 100\%
$$

### Distribution Coverage Ratio

SURE provides a fixed annual distribution rate of 9%, equivalent to 0.75% per month.

Distribution coverage is measured as:

$$
\mathrm{Distribution\ Coverage\ Ratio}
=
\frac{
\mathrm{Net\ Return\ Available\ for\ Distribution}
}{
\mathrm{Distribution\ Paid}
}
$$

| Coverage Ratio | Interpretation |
| :-- | :-- |
| Above 1.0x | Net return exceeded the distribution amount |
| Equal to 1.0x | Net return matched the distribution amount |
| Below 1.0x | The distribution exceeded current-period net return |

A coverage ratio below 1.0x may indicate that the distribution was supported by retained value, reserves or capital and may reduce SURE NAV.

## Risk Limits and Response

Each risk metric is monitored against an approved operating limit.

| Metric | Measurement Scope | Limit |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Tracking Difference | SURE versus underlying Fund | Approved product limit |
| Net FX Exposure Ratio | All FX positions in aggregate | Approved Fund limit |
| Gross Leverage Ratio | All FX positions in aggregate | Approved Fund limit |
| Single Currency Exposure Ratio | Each currency | Approved Fund limit |
| Pair Concentration Ratio | Each statistical relationship | Approved Fund limit |
| Maximum Drawdown | Underlying Fund and SURE | Approved escalation threshold |
| Distribution Coverage Ratio | SURE distribution structure | Monitored and disclosed |
| Liquidity | Portfolio and redemption reserve | Approved liquidity threshold |

<Steps>
  <Step title="Detect">
    Recalculate risk metrics using current positions, prices, balances, margin, Fund NAV and Token supply.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Investigate">
    Identify whether the change results from market movement, model drift, incomplete execution, valuation timing, liquidity or a data-quality issue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Correct">
    Add or adjust hedges, reduce positions, rebalance collateral, restrict new trades or increase available liquidity where appropriate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Escalate">
    Material or persistent breaches may result in portfolio de-risking, subscription suspension, redemption controls or other actions permitted by the product documents.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Principal Risk Categories

### Statistical Model and Convergence Risk

Currency relationships are estimated from historical and current data. They may change, weaken or fail to converge.

**Risk controls may include:**

- correlation and cointegration monitoring;
- model-drift detection;
- regime analysis;
- volatility-adjusted thresholds;
- stop-loss and time-stop rules;
- position diversification; and
- independent model review.

### Market, Leverage and Margin Risk

FX prices may change rapidly after economic data, policy decisions, intervention or geopolitical events. Leverage can magnify losses and create forced liquidation risk.

**Risk controls may include:**

- gross and net exposure limits;
- leverage and margin-utilization limits;
- stress testing;
- event-risk reduction;
- liquidation-distance monitoring; and
- portfolio de-risking after drawdown triggers.

### Execution and Liquidity Risk

Multi-leg positions may be affected by partial fills, rejected orders, slippage, widening spreads and insufficient market depth.

**Risk controls may include:**

- maximum slippage parameters;
- order-size and participation limits;
- coordinated leg execution;
- liquidity filters;
- transaction-cost analysis; and
- manual escalation for incomplete hedges.

### Counterparty, Custody and Banking Risk

The Fund and SURE depend on banks, brokers, custodians, administrators and other service providers.

**Risk controls may include:**

- due diligence before approving a service provider;
- counterparty exposure limits;
- segregation of duties;
- cash and position reconciliation;
- withdrawal and payment controls;
- incident-response procedures; and
- reducing exposure when risk indicators deteriorate.

### Stablecoin and Blockchain Risk

USDC, USDT and supported blockchain networks may experience depegging, liquidity stress, issuer restrictions, congestion, software failure or security incidents.

**Risk controls may include:**

- approved stablecoin and network lists;
- stablecoin concentration limits;
- wallet and address allowlists;
- multisignature controls;
- verified smart-contract addresses;
- emergency-pause procedures; and
- conversion and rebalancing plans.

### Liquidity and Redemption Risk

Redemption settlement depends on available product cash, the underlying Fund's dealing cycle and the ability to value and liquidate positions.

**Risk controls may include:**

- maintaining a liquidity reserve;
- estimating exit time and market impact;
- limiting position size relative to executable liquidity;
- aligning subscriptions and redemptions with the Fund dealing cycle;
- redemption gates and queues; and
- suspension procedures for exceptional conditions.

## Transparency and Reporting

Transparency helps users understand how SURE tracks and reports the underlying Fund. It does not provide real-time visibility into every FX position or eliminate valuation uncertainty.

| Disclosure Area | Information Presented | Frequency or Trigger |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| SURE NAV | Official NAV per SURE and valuation date | Monthly |
| Underlying Fund NAV | Official Fund NAV used in the SURE calculation | Monthly |
| Tracking Difference | Difference between SURE and underlying Fund total return | Periodically |
| Distribution Rate | Fixed annual rate of 9% and monthly rate of 0.75% | Continuous disclosure |
| Distribution Payment | Amount, record date and payment date | Monthly |
| Distribution Coverage | Net return relative to distribution paid | Monthly or periodically |
| Portfolio Risk | Net FX exposure, gross leverage, concentration and drawdown | Periodically |
| Liquidity | Redemption status, gates and material settlement delays | Current status |
| Product Supply | Valid SURE units outstanding and mint or burn activity | Onchain and reconciled |
| Smart Contracts | Verified addresses, networks and privileged roles | At deployment and after changes |
| Material Events | Events affecting valuation, trading, custody, distributions or redemption | As reasonably practicable |
| Documents | Product terms, NAV methodology, risk disclosure and relevant policies | Current versions through official channels |

### Onchain Transparency

Onchain records may show:

- SURE issuance;
- SURE transfers;
- units pending redemption;
- SURE burns; and
- smart-contract administration events.

Onchain records do not automatically show:

- the value of the underlying Fund position;
- bank balances;
- open FX positions;
- collateral and margin;
- accrued Fund liabilities;
- distribution coverage;
- the accuracy of official NAV; or
- counterparty solvency.

<Info>
  Onchain supply data should be read together with official Fund NAV, SURE NAV, product reporting and reconciliation. Blockchain visibility is not equivalent to a complete real-time audit.
</Info>

## Important Limitations

- Risk metrics are point-in-time measurements and can change rapidly.
- Statistical relationships and hedge ratios are estimates.
- Official Fund NAV is generally monthly and may not reflect current intramonth trading.
- Risk limits may be breached before corrective action can be completed.
- Diversification reduces some concentrations but does not remove systemic risk.
- Audits, administration, custody and smart-contract reviews reduce certain risks but cannot guarantee security or performance.
- Past performance and historical drawdown do not predict future results.
