title: "ETFT Caption"
---

ETFT Caption is Alloco's infrastructure for bringing externally managed portfolios into the ETFT framework. It connects a manager's investment mandate with product issuance, execution, NAV accounting, and primary-market operations.

Manager onboarding and product launch depend on approval and the applicable operating arrangements. A description of Caption does not mean that permissionless deployment or every external strategy is supported.

## From mandate to product

![07 Caption](/images/07-caption.png "07 Caption")

1. **Manager and strategy review.** Establish the manager's identity, investment process, operational capability, and conflicts of interest.
2. **Product structure.** Identify the issuer, investor rights, manager mandate, and service agreements.
3. **Configuration.** Define eligible instruments, venues, risk limits, fees, valuation, and dealing terms.
4. **Operational validation.** Validate execution, reconciliation, unit accounting, and exception handling.
5. **Primary launch.** Introduce capital and enable primary operations under the approved product terms.
6. **Ongoing operation.** Monitor the mandate and evaluate secondary-market readiness separately.

## Manager authority

A manager can make portfolio decisions within its approved scope. That authority does not automatically include unrestricted withdrawals, unit issuance, fee changes, or changes to the NAV methodology.

Critical responsibilities are separated so that a portfolio instruction does not itself authorize asset release or creation of unbacked units.

## Product separation

Each product has its own attributable assets, liabilities, expenses, and unit accounting. Assets of one ETFT are not available to meet another product's losses or margin obligations without a specifically authorized arrangement reflected in its documents.

Legal isolation depends on the actual issuance structure and agreements, not solely on separate internal ledgers.

## Changes and termination

Manager replacement, mandate changes, restrictions, and product wind-down follow the product's governing process. Portfolio changes are recorded separately from changes to management authority.

Caption is the infrastructure. [Manager-Led ETFTs](/manager-led-etfts) are one product family that uses it. Approved rules-based and thematic products can also use the same operating framework.
