### How SURE Works<br />Sure provides onchain access to a regulated mutual fund through a simple subscription and redemption process.

1. **Confirm Regional Availability** Sure is not available to users located in, incorporated in, or ordinarily resident in restricted jurisdictions. No separate eligibility verification is required. By accessing or using SURE, users confirm that they are not subject to any applicable geographic restrictions.
2. **Connect a Compatible Wallet** Connect a supported self-custodial wallet. Users remain responsible for their private keys, transaction approvals and use of the correct blockchain network.
3. **Subscribe Using Supported Stablecoins** Enter the subscription amount and transfer a supported stablecoin. SURE does not charge a subscription fee, although blockchain network fees may apply.
4. **Allocate to the Underlying Fund Structure** The issuer attributes accepted subscription assets to SURE and arranges the corresponding investment, liquidity reserve or settlement exposure in accordance with the SURE product terms.
5. **Receive SURE at the Applicable Product NAV** Once the subscription transaction is accepted, SURE units are issued based on the applicable NAV per unit. NAV data uses UTC (UTC\+0) as the reference time zone.
6. **Receive Distributions or Continue Holding** Monthly distributions may be paid in the manner specified to the SURE terms.
7. **Request Redemption** Redemption proceeds are calculated using the applicable NAV and may be adjusted for network costs and any other expenses disclosed in the product documentation.

1. **Important Considerations**

Before subscribing to GAIN, users should review the product terms, risk disclosures and restricted-jurisdiction policy.
