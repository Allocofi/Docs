## Overview

ETFT fees are designed to be transparent and aligned with how the underlying portfolio is actually executed.

There is **no separate fee to buy an ETFT**. Investors may incur a management fee while holding the product, an early sell fee where applicable, and transaction-level costs associated with executing the underlying portfolio.

These costs fall into three categories:

1. **Product Fees** — fees charged at the ETFT level
2. **Execution Costs** — costs associated with buying or selling the underlying assets
3. **Network Costs** — blockchain transaction costs associated with onchain interactions

## Fee Summary

| Fee | Amount | How It Is Applied |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Buy Fee** | 0% | No separate Alloco fee for buying an ETFT |
| **Management Fee** | 0.5%–2.0% p.a. | Accrued over time and reflected in the product's net economics |
| **Sell Fee** | 0.10% if held for less than 3 months | Deducted from applicable sell proceeds |
| **Sell Fee after 3 months** | 0% | Waived after the applicable holding period |
| **Underlying Execution Costs** | Variable | Reflected in executable Buy/Sell pricing |
| **Network Fees** | Variable | Determined by the applicable blockchain network |

## Buy Fee

**0%**

Alloco does not charge a separate product fee when an investor buys an ETFT.

However, buying an ETFT requires the corresponding underlying portfolio to be established through the integrated brokerage infrastructure.

As a result:

$$
Buy\ Fee = 0
$$

while the executable transaction price may still reflect the actual cost of acquiring the underlying assets.

<Info>
  A 0% Buy Fee does not mean that the underlying market transaction has zero cost. Brokerage, spreads, execution costs, and other applicable transaction-level costs may still affect the amount of ETFT received.
</Info>

## Management Fee

Each ETFT charges an annual management fee ranging from:

**0.5%–2.0% p.a.**

The applicable rate depends on the individual ETFT and is disclosed on its product page.

The management fee compensates for ongoing product management, including portfolio methodology, monitoring, rebalancing, operational infrastructure, and product administration.

The fee is accrued over time rather than charged as a separate upfront payment when an investor buys an ETFT.

Conceptually, a daily management fee accrual may be represented as:

$$
Fee_{daily}
=
NAV_{gross}
\times
\frac{m}{365}
$$

where:

- $$NAV_{gross}$$ = applicable gross product value
- $$m$$ = annual management fee rate
- $$Fee_{daily}$$ = applicable daily fee accrual

Management fees are **not included in the public Indicative NAV (iNAV)**, which is designed to represent the gross performance of the reference portfolio.

They are reflected in the economics of the investable ETFT through the Net Product NAV.

## Sell Fee

ETFTs use a holding-period-based sell fee.

| Holding Period | Sell Fee |
| :-- | --: |
| **Less than 3 months** | 0.10% |
| **3 months or longer** | 0% |

For an applicable sell transaction:

$$
Sell\ Fee
=
Gross\ Sell\ Proceeds
\times
Fee\ Rate
$$

For example, if an ETFT position is subject to the 0.10% sell fee, the applicable fee is deducted from the proceeds of the transaction before the final claimable amount is determined.

<Note>
  The methodology used to determine the applicable holding period is specified in the relevant ETFT product documentation.
</Note>

## Underlying Execution Costs

ETFTs are **execution-backed products**.

When an investor buys or sells an ETFT, corresponding transactions are executed in the underlying securities through Alloco's integrated brokerage infrastructure.

These transactions may generate costs that vary depending on the portfolio, order size, trading session, and prevailing market conditions.

### Brokerage Execution Fees

Underlying U.S. equity execution may be subject to brokerage charges based on factors such as:

- Number of shares executed
- Applicable execution tier
- Trading session
- Order type
- Characteristics of the underlying security

Execution outside the regular U.S. market session, including pre-market, post-market, and overnight trading, may be subject to a different brokerage cost structure.

These costs are not charged as a separate Alloco Buy Fee. Where applicable, they are incorporated into the executable pricing of the ETFT transaction.

### Market Spreads and Slippage

Bid-ask spreads and slippage may also affect execution.

These are **not fees charged by Alloco or the broker**. They arise from the prices available in the underlying market when orders are executed.

For example:

$$
Execution\ Cost
=
Explicit\ Brokerage\ Costs
+
Implicit\ Market\ Costs
$$

where implicit market costs may include bid-ask spread, slippage, and market impact.

### Regulatory and Third-Party Costs

Certain underlying transactions may also be subject to regulatory, exchange, corporate-action, or other third-party charges.

Where applicable to an ETFT transaction, these costs may be passed through as part of the underlying execution economics.

## Stablecoin Conversion

ETFTs are traded using **USDC**, while the underlying U.S. securities are priced and executed in **USD**.

Where conversion between USDC and USD is required as part of the execution process, applicable conversion costs may be incorporated into the transaction economics.

Any such cost forms part of the execution process rather than a separate ETFT Buy Fee.

## Network Fees

ETFTs operate across supported blockchain networks, including:

- **Ethereum**
- **BNB Chain**

Wallet interactions may require blockchain network fees, including when:

- Confirming a Buy
- Confirming a Sell
- Claiming ETFT tokens
- Claiming USDC
- Transferring supported tokens

Network fees are determined by the applicable blockchain and current network conditions. They are not charged or controlled by Alloco.

---

## How Fees Affect a Buy

For a Buy transaction, the relationship can be expressed conceptually as:

$$
USDC
\rightarrow
Underlying\ Execution
\rightarrow
ETFT
$$

with:

$$
Net\ Executable\ Value
=
USDC\ Amount
-
Underlying\ Execution\ Costs
$$

There is no additional Alloco Buy Fee.

The final ETFT amount is determined by the actual execution economics and applicable ETFT pricing methodology.

---

## How Fees Affect a Sell

For a Sell transaction:

$$
ETFT
\rightarrow
Underlying\ Liquidation
\rightarrow
USDC
$$

and:

$$
Net\ Sell\ Proceeds
=
Gross\ Execution\ Proceeds
-
Execution\ Costs
-
Applicable\ Sell\ Fee
$$

The resulting amount becomes available to the investor once the transaction reaches **Claimable** status.

---

## What Is Not a Fee?

It is important to distinguish fees from changes caused by market execution.

The following may affect the final amount received but are not Alloco product fees:

- Bid-ask spreads
- Market price movements
- Slippage
- Market impact
- Differences between Indicative NAV and executable prices
- Extended-hours liquidity conditions

These factors are inherent to executing the underlying portfolio and may vary from transaction to transaction.

  For a detailed explanation of the relationship between Indicative NAV, Net Product NAV, and executable prices, see **Pricing & NAV**.
