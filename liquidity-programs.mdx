---
title: "Liquidity programs"
---

Eligible users can participate directly in primary creation and redemption. Access is not reserved solely for investment banks or large broker-dealers, but remains subject to the product's participation and transfer rules.

Alloco primary participation is distinct from authorization to create traditional ETF shares or third-party tokenized stocks. Using an ETFT does not automatically grant access to an external issuer's authorized participant network.

## Incentive principles

Any participation program is published separately with its eligibility, measurement period, fixed budget, reward form, caps, and settlement terms. No standing reward entitlement is created by these docs.

Programs focus on sustained capital or executable liquidity rather than raw mint counts, wallet counts, or gross trading volume.

| Program focus | Eligible contribution | Excluded behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary participation | Retained net capital over a defined period | Short-cycle mint-and-redeem activity designed to farm rewards |
| Liquidity provision | Two-sided executable depth within defined pricing limits | Unusable quoted depth or inventory that is not actually available |
| Execution quality | Measurable improvement in trading costs for specified order sizes | Self-trading, wash volume, and related-party volume manipulation |

## Funding and distribution

Programs can use capped fee credits or rebates against charges actually incurred. A rebate against a zero fee has no economic value and is not presented as a cash return. Points, if used, do not imply a token allocation, cash value, or investment return unless separate published terms expressly provide one.

Rewards funded by a product are included in its disclosed expenses and NAV accounting. Externally funded programs have their own budget and do not silently charge existing holders.

## Liquidity quality

A program may score the smaller of bid-side and ask-side executable depth, adjusted for spread and valid quoting time. This rewards balanced liquidity rather than one-sided inventory. Market-open and market-closed periods can be evaluated separately.

Program eligibility does not imply that a liquidity provider is protected from inventory losses, adverse selection, or price impact.
