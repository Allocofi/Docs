## Overview

Selling an ETFT converts your ETFT position back into USDC through the execution of its underlying portfolio.

Enter the amount of ETFT you want to sell, review the real-time quote, and confirm the transaction. The corresponding underlying assets are then sold through Alloco's integrated brokerage infrastructure.

Once the underlying execution is complete, the resulting USDC becomes available to claim directly to your wallet.

<Info>
  ETFT sales are execution-backed. The amount of USDC received is determined by the actual execution of the underlying portfolio, applicable transaction costs, and any applicable sell fee.
</Info>

## Step 1 — Select an ETFT

Open your portfolio and select the ETFT you want to sell.

Before selling, you can review information including:

- ETFT balance
- Indicative NAV
- Current portfolio holdings
- Performance
- Holding period
- Applicable sell fee

You can choose to sell all or part of your ETFT position.

## Step 2 — Enter Your Sell Amount

Enter the amount of ETFT tokens you want to sell.

For example:

> **You Sell:** 100 Pelosi ETFT<br />**You Receive:** ≥ \[Quoted Amount\] USDC

Alloco generates a real-time quote showing the **minimum amount of USDC expected to be received** from the transaction.

The quote takes into account:

- Current prices of the underlying assets
- Current ETFT portfolio composition
- Indicative NAV
- Expected underlying execution
- Applicable brokerage and transaction costs
- Current market and liquidity conditions
- Applicable sell fee

<Info>
  The quote is generated before the underlying portfolio is sold. Final transaction economics reflect the actual execution of the underlying assets.
</Info>

## Step 3 — Review the Sell Fee

The applicable sell fee depends on how long the ETFT position has been held.

| Holding Period | Sell Fee |
| :-- | --: |
| **Less than 3 months** | 0.10% |
| **3 months or longer** | 0% |

Any applicable sell fee is reflected in the transaction before completion.

<Note>
  The applicable holding period is determined according to the methodology specified in the relevant product documentation.
</Note>

## Step 4 — Confirm the Sell

Review the transaction details and click **Sell**.

Confirm the transaction with your wallet to initiate the sale.

Once confirmed, the order enters:

**Processing**

At this stage, the corresponding underlying positions are routed through Alloco's integrated brokerage infrastructure for execution.

## Step 5 — Underlying Execution

Selling an ETFT results in the corresponding portion of its underlying portfolio being sold.

Using **Pelosi ETFT** as an example, the portfolio currently contains positions including GOOGL, AMZN, AVGO, NVDA, AAPL, V, MSFT, CRM, and other securities.

Conceptually, the amount of each underlying asset to be liquidated is determined by:

$$
Liquidation\ Notional_i = Portfolio\ Value_{\text{sold}} \times Weight_i
$$

where:

- $$Liquidation\ Notional_i$$ = target value of underlying asset $$i$$ to be sold
- $$Portfolio\ Value_{\text{sold}}$$ = underlying portfolio value corresponding to the ETFT amount being sold
- $$Weight_i$$ = applicable portfolio weight of underlying asset $$i$$

The actual proceeds depend on the prices achieved when the underlying orders are executed.

Execution may be affected by:

- Market movement
- Bid-ask spreads
- Available liquidity
- Partial or fractional execution
- Extended-hours market conditions
- Brokerage and transaction costs

These execution results are incorporated through the ETFT's pass-through pricing methodology.

## Step 6 — USDC Becomes Claimable

Once the required underlying assets have been successfully sold, the order status changes from:

**Processing → Claimable**

The resulting USDC is now ready to claim.

The claimable amount reflects the completed underlying execution, applicable transaction costs, and any applicable sell fee.

## Step 7 — Claim Your USDC

Click **Claim** and confirm the transaction with your wallet.

The corresponding USDC will be transferred to your connected wallet on the supported network.

Once the claim is successfully completed, the order status changes to:

**Completed**

The sell transaction is now fully settled.

## Order Lifecycle

| Status | What Happens |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Quote** | A real-time estimate of the minimum USDC amount is generated. |
| **Processing** | The sell has been confirmed and the corresponding underlying assets are being sold. |
| **Claimable** | Underlying execution is complete and USDC is ready to claim. |
| **Completed** | USDC has been claimed to your wallet and the transaction is fully settled. |

## Important Considerations

<Warning>
  The indicative NAV is not a guaranteed execution price. Market prices may change between quote generation and execution, and the final transaction economics may be affected by liquidity, bid-ask spreads, extended-hours conditions, and underlying execution costs.
</Warning>

Selling an ETFT requires execution of the corresponding underlying portfolio. Under stressed market conditions, market closures, trading halts, insufficient liquidity, broker interruptions, or other exceptional circumstances, execution may be delayed or otherwise affected.

The availability of the **Claim** action depends on successful completion of the required underlying execution.
