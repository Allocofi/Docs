## Product Types

Leveraging our native ETFT infrastructure, Alloco develops three specialized categories of financial products tailored to dynamic market demand. Products of this nature were historically the exclusive domain of traditional asset management giants like BlackRock and State Street. Through Alloco’s smart contract architecture, these structured strategies are now fully programmable, transparent, and accessible directly on-chain.

## How It Works

ETFT connects onchain assets with real-world market execution. From selecting a product to receiving ETFT tokens or stablecoins, each trade follows a transparent execution and settlement process.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose an ETFT & Connect Your Wallet">
    Explore ETFT based on your investment preferences and risk profile. Review the underlying portfolio, strategy, performance, and key risks, then connect your wallet to begin trading.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get a Quote">
    For a **Buy**, enter the amount of stablecoins you want to invest. The system provides a quote showing the **minimum amount of ETFT tokens you are expected to receive**.

    For a **Sell**, enter the amount of ETFT tokens you want to sell. The system provides a quote showing the **minimum amount of stablecoins you are expected to receive**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Execute — Processing">
    Click **Buy** or **Sell** and confirm the transaction. The corresponding order is submitted to Alpaca, where the underlying assets are bought or sold.

    While the underlying trade is being executed, the order status is shown as **Processing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim — Claimable">
    Once the underlying asset execution has been successfully completed, the order status changes to **Claimable**.

    Interact with your wallet again to claim your assets:

    - **Buy:** Receive the corresponding ETFT tokens.
    - **Sell:** Receive the corresponding stablecoins.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Settlement — Completed">
    Once the assets have been successfully claimed to your wallet, the order status changes to **Completed**. The transaction is now fully settled.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Order Status

| Status | What It Means |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Processing** | Your order has been submitted and the underlying assets are being executed. |
| **Claimable** | Underlying execution is complete and your ETFT tokens or stablecoins are ready to claim. |
| **Completed** | The assets have been claimed and the transaction is fully settled. |
