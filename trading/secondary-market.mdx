SURE is an onchain investment product issued by **Allocofi Limited** and made available through Alloco. It is designed to provide eligible users with economic exposure to the **Trivesta Global Allocation Fund**, a private sub-fund of Trivesta OFC.

The underlying fund follows a flexible global multi-strategy mandate. Its current product materials emphasize foreign-exchange trading and precious metals, including gold, supported by systematic execution, active position management and a defined risk-management framework.

<Info>
  SURE is a separate onchain product. Holding SURE does not, by itself, make a holder a direct shareholder of Trivesta OFC, the Trivesta Global Allocation Fund or any Cayman master fund. Holder rights arise from the definitive SURE product terms and the obligations of the SURE issuer.
</Info>

## Underlying Fund Classes

The Trivesta Global Allocation Fund currently describes three USD classes:

| Underlying Class | Minimum Initial Subscription at Fund Level | Annualized Monthly Distribution Objective |
| :-- | --: | --: |
| Class A | USD 100,000 | 8% |
| Class B | USD 300,000 | 9% |
| Class C | USD 1,000,000 | 10% |

The minimum subscription displayed for SURE may differ because SURE is an issuer product that may aggregate subscriptions before investing at the underlying-fund level. The applicable SURE series, target distribution and minimum amount are confirmed in the application before subscription.

## How SURE Works

1. **Complete Eligibility and Compliance Review**
   SURE is available only where lawful and only to users who satisfy the applicable investor, sanctions, AML and wallet-screening requirements.
2. **Connect a Compatible Wallet**
   Connect a supported self-custodial wallet. Users remain responsible for their private keys, transaction approvals and use of the correct blockchain network.
3. **Subscribe with a Supported Stablecoin**
   Transfer the supported subscription asset shown in the application. Network fees, conversion costs and any SURE-specific fees are disclosed before confirmation.
4. **Allocate to the Underlying Fund Structure**
   The issuer attributes accepted subscription assets to SURE and arranges the corresponding investment, liquidity reserve or settlement exposure in accordance with the SURE product terms.
5. **Receive SURE at the Applicable Product NAV**
   SURE units are minted after the subscription is accepted and the applicable valuation and reconciliation checks are completed.
6. **Receive Distributions or Continue Holding**
   Monthly distributions may be paid in the manner specified for the relevant SURE series. A distribution is not the same as investment profit and may reduce the product NAV.
7. **Request Redemption**
   Standard redemptions follow the underlying monthly liquidity cycle. Accelerated redemption may be available from issuer liquidity, subject to limits, pricing, fees and market conditions.
