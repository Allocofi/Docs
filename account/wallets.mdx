## From Strategy to Onchain Product

**ETFT Launcher** is Alloco's product infrastructure for transforming an investment idea, portfolio, or systematic methodology into a structured Onchain ETFT.

Instead of building portfolio logic, pricing infrastructure, brokerage execution, smart contracts, and investor settlement independently, creators can define the investment methodology while Alloco provides the infrastructure required to operate the ETFT throughout its lifecycle.

> **Investment Idea → Portfolio Methodology → Product Structure → Execution Infrastructure → Onchain ETFT**

ETFT Launcher is designed for strategy managers, asset managers, research teams, communities, and other qualified product creators seeking to bring a defined investment exposure onchain.

## End-to-End Architecture

An ETFT begins with a defined investment methodology. Alloco converts that methodology into an operational product configuration that connects portfolio logic with both traditional-market execution and onchain settlement.

The product lifecycle can be summarized as:

1. **Define** — Establish the investment idea and product objective.
2. **Configure** — Set assets, weights, methodology, fees, and risk controls.
3. **Review** — Assess execution, data, operational, and distribution feasibility.
4. **Build** — Configure pricing, execution, and smart contract infrastructure.
5. **Test** — Validate Buy, Sell, NAV, execution, and Claim workflows.
6. **Launch** — Make the ETFT available through approved distribution channels.
7. **Manage** — Operate rebalancing, corporate actions, reporting, and product updates.

## Creator vs. Infrastructure

ETFT Launcher separates the investment methodology from the infrastructure required to operate it.

| Creator / Strategy Provider | Alloco Infrastructure |
| :-- | :-- |
| Investment thesis | Product configuration |
| Portfolio methodology | Pricing and NAV engine |
| Asset selection inputs | Brokerage integration |
| Weighting and rebalancing rules | Execution workflow |
| Strategy data and intellectual property | Smart contracts and onchain settlement |
| Methodology updates | Product operations and reporting |

The exact allocation of legal, operational, investment-management, and distribution responsibilities is determined by the structure and documentation of each product.

## What Launcher Is Not

ETFT Launcher is not a guarantee that any proposed strategy can be launched or distributed in every jurisdiction.

Every proposed ETFT remains subject to applicable asset eligibility, technical feasibility, liquidity, legal, compliance, and distribution review before launch.
