# **What Is an Onchain ETFT?**

**The simplicity of an ETF. The power of onchain markets.**<br />An **Onchain ETF Token (ETFT)** brings the portfolio structure and simplicity of an ETF onchain, packaging a diversified basket of assets, systematic strategy, or market theme into a single tokenized product. Powered by smart contracts, each ETFT integrates predefined portfolio rules, programmatic rebalancing, real-time NAV tracking, minting and redemption and continuous liquidity—giving investors a simpler way to access and manage diversified exposure through a single token.
