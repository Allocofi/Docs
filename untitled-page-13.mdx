## Overview

ETFT Launcher provides product and distribution infrastructure, but launching an onchain product does not by itself make that product suitable or lawful for distribution in every jurisdiction.

Each ETFT must be considered in light of its specific issuer, underlying assets, legal structure, target investors, distribution channels, and applicable laws and regulations.

<Warning>
  Blockchain availability should not be interpreted as universal eligibility. A wallet's technical ability to interact with an ETFT does not necessarily mean that the holder is legally eligible to acquire or trade the product.
</Warning>

## Product Review

Before launch, a proposed ETFT may be reviewed across areas including:

- Product structure
- Underlying assets
- Investment methodology
- Target investors
- Target jurisdictions
- Distribution channels
- Marketing materials
- Tokenholder rights
- Required risk disclosures
- Data and intellectual-property rights

The required review depends on the characteristics of the individual product.

## Issuer & Product Structure

The applicable product documents should clearly identify the legal structure of the ETFT, including where relevant:

- Product issuer
- Strategy or methodology provider
- Operator or administrator
- Brokerage and execution arrangements
- Nature of the ETFT token
- Rights associated with the token
- Relationship to underlying assets
- Governing terms

The term **ETFT** describes Alloco's product format. It should not be interpreted, by itself, as establishing the regulatory classification of a product in any jurisdiction.

## Investor Eligibility

Eligibility may depend on factors such as:

- Investor jurisdiction
- Product classification
- Investor type or status
- Applicable offering restrictions
- Sanctions or restricted-person screening
- Distribution-channel requirements

Where required, product access may be limited to eligible users or supported jurisdictions.

## KYC / AML

Applicable onboarding or transaction controls may include identity verification, sanctions screening, anti-money-laundering checks, or other compliance procedures.

Requirements may vary by product, issuer, jurisdiction, and distribution channel.

Successful wallet connection should not be treated as completion of any legally required eligibility or compliance process.

## Restricted Jurisdictions

Certain ETFTs may not be offered, distributed, or made available in particular jurisdictions.

Product creators and distribution partners should ensure that marketing and access controls are consistent with the applicable product documentation and distribution restrictions.

Restrictions may change over time in response to laws, regulation, sanctions, product structure, or service-provider requirements.

## Product Disclosures

Before distribution, investors should have access to sufficient information to understand the ETFT and its material risks.

Depending on the product, disclosures may include:

- Product overview
- Key product terms
- Underlying holdings
- Investment methodology
- Rebalancing methodology
- Pricing and NAV methodology
- Fees
- Risk factors
- Tokenholder rights
- Conflicts of interest
- Legal and eligibility information

Product-specific disclosures should take precedence over general descriptions of ETFT Launcher.

## Public Figures & Third-Party References

ETFTs may reference public figures, investment managers, companies, institutions, indices, communities, or other third parties.

Unless expressly stated otherwise, reference to a third party does not mean that the third party:

- Sponsors the ETFT
- Endorses the ETFT
- Manages the ETFT
- Advises Alloco
- Approves the methodology
- Participates in product distribution

Creators are responsible for identifying and addressing applicable rights, data licenses, trademark considerations, publicity rights, and marketing restrictions associated with third-party references.

## Data & Intellectual Property

Creators should have the rights and permissions necessary to provide and use any proprietary strategy, index, dataset, signal, portfolio methodology, branding, or other intellectual property incorporated into an ETFT.

The use of public information does not automatically remove all legal, contractual, licensing, attribution, or intellectual-property considerations.

## Distribution

Approved ETFTs may be made available through supported onchain and partner distribution channels, subject to the applicable product and jurisdictional requirements.

Distribution infrastructure may support:

- Wallet-based access
- Onchain ownership
- Product discovery
- Real-time Buy and Sell workflows
- NAV and portfolio reporting
- Partner integrations

Distribution availability can differ by product and should not be interpreted as a guarantee of liquidity or universal market access.

## Roles & Responsibilities

Responsibilities depend on the structure of each ETFT and the agreements between the relevant parties.

A typical allocation may include:

| Creator / Strategy Provider | Alloco |
| :-- | :-- |
| Define investment idea | Configure ETFT infrastructure |
| Provide methodology and strategy inputs | Operate pricing and NAV infrastructure |
| Provide or license required strategy data | Integrate brokerage execution |
| Maintain strategy-specific intellectual property | Provide smart contract infrastructure |
| Identify methodology changes | Support onchain transaction lifecycle |
| Provide strategy-specific disclosures and inputs | Provide product operations and reporting infrastructure |

This table is illustrative only. The definitive allocation of responsibilities is governed by the applicable agreements and product documentation.

## Marketing & Communications

Marketing materials should accurately describe the ETFT and remain consistent with its governing product documentation.

Communications should avoid presenting:

- Historical performance as guaranteed future performance
- Indicative NAV as a guaranteed executable price
- Public-figure references as endorsements where no endorsement exists
- Blockchain accessibility as universal legal eligibility
- Risk controls as eliminating investment risk
- The term ETFT as proof of regulatory status

## Ongoing Compliance

Compliance is an ongoing process rather than a one-time launch step.

Material changes to a product may require renewed review, including changes to:

- Investment methodology
- Underlying assets
- Product fees
- Issuer or service providers
- Distribution jurisdictions
- Marketing approach
- Tokenholder rights
- Smart contract functionality

Product creators and relevant service providers should maintain appropriate processes for identifying and addressing changes that may affect the ETFT's legal, operational, or distribution requirements.

## Important Notice

The information in these Docs provides a general description of ETFT Launcher and does not replace the definitive legal, offering, or product documentation applicable to an individual ETFT.

Where there is any inconsistency, the applicable definitive product documentation governs.
