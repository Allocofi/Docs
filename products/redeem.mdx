## Overview

Redeeming an ETFT converts your units into USDC through the primary-market redemption process.

Enter the units to redeem, review the estimated USDC proceeds and applicable fees, and confirm your request. The corresponding underlying positions are then reduced or liquidated under the product's execution methodology.

**Redemptions settle on T+1, the next trading day after the effective redemption date.** The amount displayed when you submit a request is an estimate, not a guaranteed payout. Final proceeds reflect actual execution, applicable costs and fees, and reconciliation.

Redemption proceeds are delivered in USDC, including when the original mint was funded with USDT.

<Tip>
  T is the U.S. securities trading day on which the redemption request becomes effective. T+1 is the next U.S. securities trading day, excluding weekends and U.S. securities market holidays. Trading dates are determined in U.S. Eastern Time (ET).
</Tip>

## 1. Quote

Reviewing an estimate does not submit a redemption request.

### Select an ETFT

Open your portfolio and select the ETFT to redeem. Review your available balance, Indicative Product NAV (iNAV), holding period, applicable redemption fee, and product risks.

You may redeem all or part of your position, subject to the product's dealing terms. Any redemption minimum or full-balance exception is disclosed before confirmation.

### Enter your redemption amount

For example:

```text
You redeem: 100 ETFT units
Estimated proceeds: [Estimated amount] USDC
```

The estimate uses available portfolio and pricing information, expected execution costs, and the applicable redemption fee.

**Estimated proceeds are indicative only. The final USDC amount may be higher or lower.** Market prices, liquidity, execution results, conversion rates, and applicable charges may change before the request is settled. Neither iNAV nor the displayed estimate guarantees an executable price or final redemption amount.

### Review the redemption fee

| Holding period | Redemption fee |
| --- | --: |
| Less than 3 months | 0.10% |
| 3 months or longer | 0% |

Holding-period measurement and fee calculation follow the relevant product methodology, including the treatment of multiple acquisition lots.

A zero redemption fee does not eliminate execution, conversion, settlement, or network costs.

## 2. Processing

Review the details, click **Redeem**, and confirm the required wallet transaction.

The request enters **Processing**, and the submitted units are reserved against it to prevent duplicate redemption or transfer. The corresponding underlying positions are reduced or liquidated, and the resulting proceeds, liabilities, and applicable costs are reconciled.

Actual proceeds depend on execution prices and may be affected by market movements, spreads, liquidity, partial fills, trading-session conditions, and transaction charges.

### T+1 settlement

Under normal market and operating conditions, redemption proceeds settle on **T+1**:

- **T** is the trading day on which the redemption request becomes effective under the applicable dealing window.
- **T+1** is the next trading day, excluding weekends and applicable market holidays.

For example, a request effective on Monday normally settles on Tuesday. A request effective on Friday normally settles on Monday, provided Monday is a trading day.

Requests submitted outside an applicable dealing window are assigned an effective redemption date under the product's dealing terms. Submission time alone does not necessarily determine T.

**T+1 refers to settlement, not a guaranteed wallet-delivery time.** Trading halts, incomplete execution, insufficient liquidity, broker interruptions, funding constraints, or other exceptional conditions may delay settlement. A delay does not automatically cancel the request.

## 3. Claimable

The request becomes **Claimable** once settlement, reconciliation, and the required funding steps are complete and USDC is available for delivery.

The interface then displays the **final claimable USDC amount**. This amount reflects actual execution, applicable conversion, allocated transaction costs, and any redemption fee. It may differ from the estimate shown when the request was submitted.

### Claim your USDC

Click **Claim** and confirm the required wallet transaction. USDC is delivered to your connected wallet on the applicable network.

Wallet issues, network congestion, or transaction-confirmation delays may affect delivery.

## 4. Completed

The request becomes **Completed** after successful USDC delivery.

The time you take to claim and the blockchain confirmation time are separate from the T+1 settlement schedule.

## Order lifecycle

| Stage | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Quote | Review estimated USDC proceeds and applicable fees. The estimate is not guaranteed. |
| Processing | Reduce underlying positions and complete execution, reconciliation, and T+1 settlement. |
| Claimable | The final confirmed USDC amount is available to claim. |
| Completed | USDC has been delivered to your wallet. |

## Important considerations

Cancellation, partial execution, failed execution, and any unwind follow the applicable product and order terms. Already executed transactions may incur costs.

USDT conversion costs incurred when minting are not reversed by later redeeming into USDC.
