## How SURE Works

1. **Connect a Compatible Wallet** Connect a supported self-custodial wallet. Users remain responsible for their private keys, transaction approvals and use of the correct blockchain network.
2. **Subscribe with a Supported Stablecoin** Transfer the supported subscription asset shown in the application. Network fees, conversion costs and any SURE-specific fees are disclosed before confirmation.
3. **Allocate to the Underlying Fund Structure** The issuer attributes accepted subscription assets to SURE and arranges the corresponding investment, liquidity reserve or settlement exposure in accordance with the SURE product terms.
4. **Receive SURE at the Applicable Product NAV** SURE units are minted after the subscription is accepted and the applicable valuation and reconciliation checks are completed.
5. **Receive Distributions or Continue Holding** Monthly distributions may be paid in the manner specified for the relevant SURE series. A distribution is not the same as investment profit and may reduce the product NAV.
6. **Request Redemption** Standard redemptions follow the underlying monthly liquidity cycle. Accelerated redemption may be available from issuer liquidity, subject to limits, pricing, fees and market conditions.

## Important Considerations

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Not a Deposit" icon="vault">
    SURE is an investment product. It is not a bank deposit, savings account or deposit-insured stable-value product.
  </Card>

  <Card title="No Guaranteed Return" icon="chart-line-down">
    Neither principal nor distributions are guaranteed. SURE NAV may fall and investors may lose some or all of their capital.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Professional-Investor Restrictions" icon="user-shield">
    The underlying fund is private and intended for professional investors. SURE must not be used to circumvent applicable eligibility or distribution restrictions.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Monthly Liquidity" icon="clock">
    The underlying fund does not provide continuous daily liquidity. Standard redemptions depend on monthly dealing dates, notice periods, valuation and settlement.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Wrapper and Counterparty Risk" icon="layer-group">
    SURE holders rely on the issuer, the underlying fund, service providers, counterparties, stablecoins and blockchain infrastructure.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Historical Performance Limitations" icon="clock-rotate-left">
