## Overview

ETFT Launcher connects an investment methodology with the infrastructure required to price, execute, and settle an Onchain ETFT.

The architecture spans both traditional financial markets and blockchain networks. Underlying securities are executed through integrated brokerage infrastructure, while ETFT ownership and investor settlement are handled through supported onchain systems.

> **Portfolio Methodology → Pricing Engine → Brokerage Execution → ETFT Smart Contract → Investor Wallet**

## Product Layer

The product layer defines what the ETFT represents and how it behaves.

It includes:

- Eligible underlying assets
- Portfolio weights
- Rebalancing rules
- Corporate-action methodology
- Product fees
- Supported networks
- Trading and settlement rules

These parameters form the operating specification used by the pricing, execution, and onchain infrastructure.

## Pricing & NAV Engine

The pricing layer maintains the relationship between the reference portfolio and the investable ETFT.

It supports:

- Indicative NAV calculation
- Corporate-action adjustments
- Net Product NAV calculations
- Buy quote generation
- Sell quote generation
- Execution-cost incorporation
- Fee calculations

The public iNAV is a reference portfolio value. Actual investor transactions use execution-adjusted pricing based on the corresponding underlying market transaction.

This separation prevents brokerage costs, spreads, or order-specific execution effects from distorting the reference performance series.

## Brokerage Execution

ETFTs are designed as **execution-backed products**.

When an investor confirms a Buy, the system generates the corresponding underlying basket and routes the required orders through supported brokerage infrastructure.

When an investor confirms a Sell, the process operates in reverse and the corresponding underlying exposure is liquidated.

Execution may involve multiple underlying orders because a single ETFT can represent a portfolio of multiple securities.

The transaction remains **Processing** while the required underlying execution is being completed.

## Smart Contract Infrastructure

The onchain layer manages the ETFT transaction lifecycle on supported networks.

Core functions may include:

- ETFT token issuance
- Token accounting
- Buy and Sell transaction state
- Claim logic
- Supported asset settlement
- Product-level access controls, where applicable

ETFTs are currently designed to support:

- **Ethereum**
- **BNB Chain**

Network support may vary by product.

## Buy Infrastructure

At a high level, a Buy follows this sequence:

1. Investor enters an amount of USDC.
2. The pricing engine generates a quote and minimum expected ETFT output.
3. Investor confirms the Buy onchain.
4. The underlying basket is submitted for execution.
5. The order remains **Processing** during execution.
6. Successful execution makes the ETFT **Claimable**.
7. Investor claims the ETFT to the connected wallet.
8. The transaction becomes **Completed**.

## Sell Infrastructure

A Sell follows the reverse sequence:

1. Investor enters an amount of ETFT to sell.
2. The pricing engine generates a quote and minimum expected USDC output.
3. Investor confirms the Sell onchain.
4. The corresponding underlying exposure is submitted for liquidation.
5. The order remains **Processing** during execution.
6. Successful execution makes the resulting USDC **Claimable**.
7. Investor claims USDC to the connected wallet.
8. The transaction becomes **Completed**.

## Onchain and Offchain Boundaries

An ETFT combines components operating in different environments.

| Component | Environment |
| :-- | :-- |
| Portfolio methodology | Product infrastructure |
| Market prices and underlying execution | Traditional market / brokerage infrastructure |
| iNAV and executable quote calculation | Pricing infrastructure |
| ETFT token | Blockchain |
| Wallet confirmation and Claim | Blockchain |
| U.S. equity settlement | Traditional financial-market infrastructure |

Onchain transparency does not mean that every component of underlying securities trading or settlement occurs onchain.

## Testing & Controls

Before a product is enabled for live distribution, Alloco validates the interaction between the product, pricing, execution, and settlement layers.

Testing may include:

- Portfolio calculations
- NAV calculations
- Quote generation
- Underlying basket construction
- Order execution
- Execution reconciliation
- Smart contract state transitions
- Claim settlement
- Failed or interrupted transaction handling

The objective is to validate the complete product lifecycle rather than only the token contract.

## Infrastructure Dependencies

ETFT operation depends on third-party and external infrastructure, which may include market data providers, brokers, trading venues, blockchain networks, stablecoin infrastructure, and other service providers.

Service interruptions, market closures, trading halts, network congestion, or other external conditions may affect ETFT availability or execution.

See **Risk & Disclosures** in the Onchain ETFT documentation for additional information.
