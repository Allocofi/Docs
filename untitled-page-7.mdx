SURE combines an onchain token, an issuer, stablecoin and banking settlement, a Hong Kong private fund, a master-fund and trading structure, and multiple service providers. Risk controls are intended to identify and reduce risk; they cannot eliminate losses or guarantee uninterrupted subscriptions, distributions or redemptions.

## Principal Risks

| Risk | Why It Matters |
| :-- | :-- |
| Market Risk | FX, gold, equities, rates, credit, commodities and other permitted investments can move adversely and cause NAV losses |
| Strategy and Model Risk | Trading assumptions, signals, hedges or discretionary decisions may fail or behave differently in changing market regimes |
| Leverage and Derivatives Risk | Margin, borrowing and derivatives can magnify losses, create liquidation risk and produce nonlinear exposures |
| Currency Risk | SURE is USD-based, but the underlying fund may invest in multiple currencies and incur conversion and hedging costs |
| Liquidity Risk | The underlying fund deals monthly; positions or fund interests may not be liquidated at the expected price or time |
| Redemption Risk | Requests may be gated, deferred, suspended, settled in kind or delayed by incomplete documentation or operational conditions |
| Distribution Risk | The 8%, 9% and 10% figures are objectives. Payments may reduce NAV and are not guaranteed |
| Performance-Fee Asymmetry | Holders bear downside, while performance above the applicable hurdle may be captured entirely by the investment manager |
| Counterparty and Master-Fund Risk | SURE depends on the underlying fund, master-fund structure, brokers, banks, trading counterparties and service providers |
| Custody and Banking Risk | Assets or cash may be frozen, delayed, misappropriated, subject to insolvency or unavailable during an operational incident |
| Valuation Risk | Some assets may be difficult to value; official NAV may be delayed, estimated or corrected |
| Issuer and Wrapper Risk | SURE holders have a claim against the SURE issuer under the product terms and may not have a direct claim on underlying assets |
| Stablecoin Risk | Subscription or redemption assets may depeg, become illiquid or face issuer, banking, sanctions or redemption constraints |
| Blockchain and Smart-Contract Risk | Contracts, wallets, bridges, networks, oracles and privileged roles may fail or be compromised |
| Operational and Cyber Risk | Human error, system outages, reconciliation failures, fraud, cyber incidents or vendor failures may affect the product |
| Legal, Regulatory and Tax Risk | Laws, licensing classifications, sanctions, public-offer restrictions and tax treatment may change |
| Conflict-of-Interest Risk | Issuer, product manager, investment manager and service providers may face conflicts in valuation, fees, allocation and liquidity decisions |
| Track-Record Risk | Historical account data and illustrative tables may be incomplete, unaudited or not attributable to the live fund or SURE |

## Risk Controls

Risk controls may include:

- professional-investor and jurisdictional eligibility checks;
- AML, sanctions, PEP, adverse-media and wallet screening;
- product-level asset and liability segregation in books and records;
- bank, wallet and underlying-fund reconciliation;
- multisignature control over privileged smart-contract functions;
- limits on accelerated-redemption liquidity;
- stablecoin and counterparty concentration limits;
- stress testing of market, leverage, redemption and stablecoin scenarios;
- review of official underlying NAV and administrator statements;
- independent legal, audit, custody and administration arrangements where applicable;
- incident-response and business-continuity procedures; and
- suspension powers for legal, security, valuation or liquidity events.

<Warning>
  A limit may be exceeded before corrective action can be completed, particularly during fast markets, bank closures, blockchain congestion, counterparty failure or data outages.
</Warning>

## Transparency Framework

| Disclosure Area | Information | Frequency or Trigger |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| SURE NAV | Official product NAV, valuation date and whether the value is official or indicative | Monthly official; indicative updates when available |
| Underlying NAV | Latest administrator-confirmed NAV used by SURE | Monthly or when corrected |
| Distributions | Amount, record date, payment date and treatment in NAV | Monthly when declared |
| Product Supply | Valid SURE units outstanding, minted and burned | Onchain and reconciled periodically |
| Product Assets | Aggregated underlying fund value, attributed cash and material receivables | Periodically, subject to confidentiality |
| Product Liabilities | Accrued fees, redemptions, distributions, expenses and reserves | Reflected in NAV; summarized periodically |
| Liquidity | Standard and accelerated redemption status, gates and material delays | Current status and material-event updates |
| Service Providers | Current issuer, manager, administrator, custodians, auditor and legal adviser | At launch and after material changes |
| Smart Contracts | Verified addresses, networks, privileged roles, multisignature and upgrade controls | At deployment and after material changes |
| Material Events | Events materially affecting valuation, security, custody, trading or redemption | As reasonably practicable |
| Governing Documents | Product terms, risk disclosure, NAV methodology, redemption policy and restricted-jurisdiction policy | Current versions through official channels |

## NAV and Performance Transparency

The official SURE NAV should use the latest official underlying-fund valuation and the SURE product's own cash, liabilities, fees, distributions and unit supply.

Performance should be reported from SURE NAV and distributions after applicable costs. It should not be reconstructed solely from:

- a manager's trading-account statement;
- a marketing deck;
- a gross return series;
- a separate historical account;
- an illustrative fee-adjusted table; or
- group-level experience.

<Info>
  The investment manager's corporate financial statements are not fund performance statements. Likewise, an underlying strategy record is not the actual performance of SURE.
</Info>

## Onchain Transparency

Blockchain records may show:

- SURE issuance;
- transfers between addresses;
- tokens held in redemption;
- burns; and
- contract-level administrative actions.

Blockchain records do not automatically show:

- bank balances;
- underlying fund shares;
- master-fund assets;
- broker positions;
- liabilities and expenses;
- pending subscriptions or redemptions;
- offchain side letters; or
- whether an underlying valuation is current and accurate.

<Warning>
  Onchain visibility is not equivalent to proof of reserves, a real-time audit or legal ownership of the underlying portfolio.
</Warning>

## Due-Diligence Status Requiring Confirmation

The supplied DDQ contains several time-sensitive statements that must be refreshed before launch or public publication:

1. **Cayman registration:** the DDQ stated that registration of Trivesta Fund SPC with CIMA was being applied for at the time of response.
2. **Master-level trading accounts:** the DDQ stated that accounts with Decode Global Limited and IG Bank were in the process of being established.
3. **Master-fund custody:** the supplied materials do not conclusively establish the current independent cash custodian and asset-custody arrangements at master-fund level.
4. **Administrator verification:** the materials identify Apex as administrator, but the precise scope of Apex's independent verification of master-fund assets and trade data should be confirmed.
5. **Historical performance attribution:** the DDQ states that the earlier record was not generated by the current ownership of Trivesta Capital Management (Hong Kong) Limited, and the current fund was newly established.
6. **Live SURE operations:** contract addresses, multisignature settings, stablecoin rails, fees, liquidity reserves and actual settlement service providers must be verified at deployment.

<Note>
  These items are launch and continuing due-diligence requirements. They should not be converted into positive claims unless supported by current signed agreements, regulator records or direct independent confirmation.
</Note>

## Material Events

Material events may include:

- a material NAV delay or correction;
- suspension, gate or prolonged redemption delay;
- a shortfall or change in distribution;
- stablecoin depeg or redemption restriction;
- bank, custodian, broker or counterparty failure;
- master-fund registration or legal-status change;
- administrator, custodian, auditor or manager replacement;
- smart-contract exploit or privileged-key compromise;
- sanctions or regulatory action;
- material litigation or insolvency; or
- termination or wind-down of SURE or the underlying fund.

Notices should be issued through official channels as reasonably practicable, subject to legal, security, market-abuse and confidentiality constraints.

## Important Limitations

- Risk metrics are point-in-time estimates and may change rapidly.
- Official underlying NAV is monthly and may not reflect current intramonth trading.
- Diversification across markets and counterparties reduces some concentration but does not remove correlated or systemic risk.
- Audits and administration reduce certain risks but do not guarantee asset safety, valuation accuracy or performance.
- A liquidity reserve can be exhausted.
- Smart-contract reviews cannot eliminate all code or governance risk.
- Past performance does not predict future results.

<Warning>
  SURE involves investment, market, leverage, counterparty, custody, liquidity, stablecoin, smart-contract, cybersecurity, operational, legal, regulatory and tax risks. Users may lose some or all of their invested capital.
</Warning>
