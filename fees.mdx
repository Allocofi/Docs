## Overview

ETFT fees distinguish ongoing product expenses from the costs of an individual primary-market transaction.

There is **no separate Alloco mint fee**. A position may incur an annual management fee, a holding-period-based redemption fee, and the actual costs associated with executing and settling the underlying portfolio.

This page covers **Mint** and **Redeem** transactions. Secondary-market Buy and Sell transactions follow the relevant venue's fee schedule and do not automatically use the primary redemption fee schedule.

Costs fall into three categories:

1. **Product fees and expenses:** management fees, applicable redemption fees, and disclosed expenses borne by the product.
2. **Execution and conversion costs:** costs of establishing or reducing underlying exposure and making required currency or stablecoin conversions.
3. **Network costs:** blockchain charges for supported onchain actions.

## Fee summary

| Fee or cost | Amount | How it is applied |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mint fee | **0%** | No separate Alloco product fee for minting |
| Management fee | **0.5%–2.0% p.a.** | Accrued over time and reflected in Product NAV; the product page states the applicable rate |
| Redemption fee: held less than 3 months | **0.10%** | Deducted from the relevant redemption proceeds |
| Redemption fee: held 3 months or longer | **0%** | No redemption fee after the applicable holding period |
| Underlying execution costs | Variable | Reflected in actual execution prices or separately allocated transaction charges |
| Stablecoin and currency conversion | Variable | Reflected in actual conversion rates and any separately charged conversion fees |
| Network fees | Variable | Determined by the network and the transactions required |

A zero mint fee or redemption fee does not eliminate other costs. The quote and transaction breakdown identify applicable charges and distinguish estimated amounts from final execution results.

## Mint fee

**0%**

Alloco does not charge a separate product fee when you mint an ETFT:

$$
F_{\mathrm{mint}}=0
$$

Minting nevertheless requires the underlying portfolio exposure to be established. Brokerage charges, spreads, price impact, required conversions, and other applicable costs can affect the units received.

<Info>
  A 0% mint fee means there is no separate Alloco mint charge. It does not mean that establishing the underlying portfolio is cost-free.
</Info>

## Management fee

Each ETFT has an annual management fee within the **0.5%–2.0% p.a.** range. The applicable rate is disclosed on the product page.

The management fee compensates for ongoing product management, including methodology maintenance, monitoring, rebalancing oversight, operational infrastructure, and administration. It is accrued over time rather than charged as a separate upfront mint payment.

### Accrual

Using a daily accrual and a 365-day convention, the calculation can be represented conceptually as:

$$
\Delta M_d=B_d\times\frac{m}{365}
$$

where:

| Symbol | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| (\\Delta M_d) | Management fee accrued for day (d) |
| (B_d) | Applicable total product fee base for that day |
| (m) | Annual management fee rate expressed as a decimal |

For example, an illustrative USD 1,000,000 fee base at 1% p.a. produces approximately USD 27.40 of daily accrual under this convention. The product terms define the fee base, day-count convention, and accrual timing.

### Effect on Product NAV

Accrued unpaid management fees are product liabilities. They reduce the net assets attributable to holders:

$$
NAV_t=\frac{A_t-L_t}{N_t}
$$

Here (At) is attributable product assets, (Lt) includes accrued fees and other liabilities, and (Nt) is the corresponding economic unit count.

Paying a fee that has already been accrued settles the liability; it is not a second deduction from NAV. Management fees already reflected in NAV are not deducted again as an order-level charge when minting or redeeming.

### Reference performance and product value

**Reference Portfolio Value** represents the theoretical performance of the defined reference portfolio. It excludes product management fees and actual product execution costs. Any modeled taxes, funding, financing, or trading costs are specified in the reference methodology. A series adjusted for modeled product fees is separately labeled **Fee-Adjusted Reference Portfolio Value** and remains a theoretical reference, not Product NAV.

**Product NAV** means Product NAV per Unit: actual attributable assets less liabilities, divided by the corresponding economic units outstanding. **Indicative Product NAV (iNAV)** is an intraday estimate using the latest available product records. **Daily Product NAV** is calculated for the specified daily valuation point and published after reconciliation.

Both iNAV and Daily Product NAV reflect applicable accrued management fees and other recognized product costs. Daily Product NAV is not iNAV with management fees deducted again. Deducting modeled fees from Reference Portfolio Value does not reproduce actual Product NAV, which also reflects actual positions, cash, taxes, funding, execution, and other accounting items.

## Redemption fee

The redemption fee depends on the holding period of the units being redeemed.

| Holding period | Redemption fee rate |
| --- | --: |
| Less than 3 months | 0.10% |
| 3 months or longer | 0% |

For units subject to a single fee rate:

$$
F_{\mathrm{redeem}}=G_{\mathrm{redeem}}\times f
$$

Gross proceeds already reflect the prices achieved in execution. Spread or slippage embedded in those prices is not added back to create an artificial gross amount or charged again as a separate deduction.

For example, gross redemption proceeds of 1,000 USDC subject to a 0.10% fee incur a 1 USDC redemption fee. Other applicable transaction costs are accounted for separately, without duplication.

Where multiple acquisition lots have different applicable rates, the fee is calculated using the gross proceeds attributable to each lot:

$$
F_{\mathrm{redeem}}=\sum_{\ell}G_{\ell}f_{\ell}
$$

<Note>
  The product methodology defines the holding-period start and end points, calendar convention, lot attribution, and treatment of transfers and secondary acquisitions. The three-month threshold does not imply a particular day count or lot-selection method unless those terms expressly specify one.
</Note>

This is a primary redemption fee. It does not automatically apply to a sale of existing ETFT units on a secondary venue. A secondary acquisition also does not automatically qualify the acquired units for fee-free redemption.

## Underlying execution costs

ETFT minting and redemption are execution-backed. Establishing or reducing the corresponding underlying portfolio can generate costs that vary by product, order size, market conditions, and trading session.

### Brokerage and venue charges

Underlying securities execution may incur charges based on the applicable brokerage arrangement, number of shares, execution tier, order type, session, and security.

Pre-market, post-market, and overnight execution may have different cost structures from regular-session execution. The applicable charges form part of the request's execution economics; they are not a separate Alloco mint fee.

For products using derivatives, venue trading fees and other relevant execution charges follow the product's implementation and fee disclosures.

### Spreads, slippage, and market impact

These are economic effects of execution, not automatically separate fees charged by Alloco or the broker.

Execution analysis may distinguish:

$$
C_{\mathrm{economic}}
=
C_{\mathrm{explicit}}+C_{\mathrm{implicit}}
$$

Explicit costs include separately charged brokerage or transaction fees. Implicit costs describe differences between an appropriate reference price and the achieved execution price.

This expression is an analytical decomposition, not an instruction to deduct every component again from actual proceeds. If actual fills already reflect spreads and price impact, those effects are not subtracted a second time. Different slippage and spread measurements can also overlap and must not be added mechanically.

### Regulatory and third-party charges

Applicable transactions may incur regulatory, exchange, settlement, or other third-party charges. Charges attributable to a particular request may be passed through under the product's execution methodology.

Expenses associated with portfolio operations, corporate actions, or taxes may instead belong to the product as a whole. Their allocation follows the product terms rather than automatically charging them to the next user who mints or redeems.

### Rebalancing and ongoing portfolio costs

A management fee does not imply that every underlying rebalance is free to execute. Where the product bears rebalancing costs or other disclosed ongoing expenses, they affect Product NAV.

For derivative-based ETFTs, funding payments may be payable or receivable, and financing or borrowing costs may apply. Their treatment follows the product methodology. Amounts already recognized in cash or liabilities must not be recorded again as additional costs.

## Stablecoin and currency conversion

### Minting with USDT

You can fund supported mints with USDC or USDT. USDT is automatically converted into USDC before the underlying execution process.

Conceptually:

$$
U_{\mathrm{USDC}}
=
U_{\mathrm{USDT}}x_{\mathrm{swap}}-K_{\mathrm{swap}}
$$

If the reported conversion output is already net of those costs, use that output directly. Do not deduct the same costs again. Conversion rates are not assumed to be one-for-one, and spreads, fees, and slippage can reduce the amount available for minting.

The 100 USDC mint minimum is tested after USDT conversion and its costs, before underlying portfolio execution costs. Network gas does not count toward the minimum.

### USDC and USD

U.S. securities are priced and executed in USD. When the funding or settlement route requires conversion between USDC and USD, the achieved rate and any applicable conversion charges form part of the transaction economics.

Costs already reflected in a net conversion rate or net settlement amount are not deducted again. A USD NAV and a USDC payment amount should not be treated as identical without applying the relevant conversion basis.

### Redemption asset

Primary redemptions deliver **USDC**, including when the original mint was funded with USDT. Earlier USDT conversion costs are not reversed by a later redemption. USDT mint support does not imply a USDT redemption option.

## Execution buffers and remaining funds

Alloco's U.S. equity execution policy uses marketable limit orders with price protection. An execution buffer provides limited room for market movement; it is **not a fee** and is not automatically charged in full.

A limit price defines the permitted execution boundary. Final pricing reflects actual fills and disclosed costs, even when execution occurs at a better price than the limit.

Any funds reserved for potential costs remain subject to reconciliation. After a successful mint, USDC that was neither used to establish the issued entitlement nor included as cash backing that entitlement is returned under the order terms. A legitimate product cash allocation remains part of the product exposure.

Unused buffer amounts are not platform revenue. See **Trading hours & order execution** for price limits, execution windows, and partial-fill handling.

## Network fees

Supported onchain actions on networks such as Ethereum and BNB Chain may require gas, including:

- Token approvals where required.
- Submitting a mint or redemption request.
- Claiming ETFT tokens or USDC.
- Transferring supported tokens.

Network fees are determined by the applicable blockchain and network conditions, rather than being an Alloco product fee. Depending on the route, separate actions can require separate transactions. A failed or reverted onchain transaction may still consume gas.

A USDC or USDT balance alone does not necessarily cover gas; the network's native token is normally required unless a supported route expressly sponsors the charge. Any separately charged service or delivery cost must be identified separately from network gas.

## How costs affect a mint

Before submission, the **Mint Quote** shows estimated ETFT output, applicable minimum output, estimated costs, and quote validity. The quote is specific to the order; neither Reference Portfolio Value nor iNAV is a guaranteed mint price.

After any required USDT conversion, the amount available to establish the corresponding product exposure reflects the actual USDC conversion output and applicable order-specific costs. Final units are established under the product's issuance and accounting methodology using the applicable product valuation and actual execution results.

The **Final Mint Outcome** identifies:

- The confirmed ETFT units issued.
- Actual conversion and execution costs attributable to the order.
- Any remaining USDC returned under the order terms.

USDC included as product cash backing the issued entitlement is distinct from unused funds returned to the investor. Reserved but unused execution buffers are not platform revenue.

Costs embedded in actual fills or net conversion amounts are not deducted again as separate charges. Accrued management fees and historical product costs already reflected in Product NAV are not charged again as the same order-level expense.

See **Pricing & NAV** for the valuation and unit-accounting principles that distinguish a Mint Quote from the Final Mint Outcome.

## How costs affect a redemption

Using gross proceeds based on actual execution and the applicable settlement conversion:

$$
U_{\mathrm{net}}
=
G_{\mathrm{redeem}}-K_{\mathrm{redeem}}-F_{\mathrm{redeem}}
$$

For illustration:

| Item | Amount |
| --- | --: |
| Gross execution-based redemption proceeds | 1,000 USDC |
| Separately deducted execution and conversion charges | 2 USDC |
| Redemption fee at 0.10% of gross proceeds | 1 USDC |
| Net redemption proceeds | **997 USDC** |

The example is hypothetical and excludes separately paid network gas. At a 0% redemption fee, the same gross proceeds and other charges would produce 998 USDC.

Net USDC becomes Claimable when the required execution, reconciliation, and funding steps are complete. An underlying fill alone does not establish that the funds are immediately available for delivery.

## Avoiding duplicate charges

The transaction and valuation methodology distinguishes costs already reflected in value from costs still to be deducted:

| Item | Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Accrued management fee | Reflected in Product NAV; not charged again on the order |
| Historical costs borne by the product | Already affect NAV; not automatically deducted again |
| Spread or slippage in actual fills | Reflected in achieved prices; not automatically a separate debit |
| Fee included in net conversion output | Not deducted again from that output |
| Separately charged order fee | Allocated once to the relevant request |
| Reserved but unused buffer | Not a fee; reconciled with the user's entitlement and any return |

Market movements, liquidity conditions, source differences, and the timing of execution can change the final amount without representing additional Alloco fees.

## Exceptions and incentives

If execution is incomplete or cannot satisfy accepted limits, the request follows the disclosed exception policy. Completed conversions or trades may generate costs even when the original request does not complete normally.

Alloco bears additional remediation costs caused by platform technical errors. Other exceptional costs follow the terms disclosed before acceptance and are not automatically transferred to other ETFT holders.

Participation rewards or fee credits do not waive charges unless the published program expressly identifies the charge and benefit. A proposed liquidity-partner arrangement is not an automatic exemption from the standard redemption schedule.
