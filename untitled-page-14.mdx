---
title: "Secondary markets"
---

Secondary trading transfers existing ETFT units between participants. Unless the route explicitly includes a primary operation, it does not create units, cancel units, or change the underlying portfolio.

|  | Primary market | Secondary market |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Transaction | Creation or redemption | Purchase or sale of existing units |
| Pricing | Product valuation and order-specific costs | Executable pool or order-book price |
| Supply | Increases or decreases | Normally unchanged |
| Timing | Asset execution and settlement process | Venue and network execution |
| Principal costs | Product, execution, conversion, and network charges | Spread, trading fee, price impact, and network charges |

## Premiums and discounts

A premium occurs when market price exceeds the relevant NAV reference. A discount occurs when it is lower.

`Premium / discount = market price / reference NAV - 1`

The market price, NAV timestamp, and underlying market state must be considered together. A difference from a stale closing NAV is not necessarily an executable arbitrage opportunity.

## How primary participation can reduce deviations

When a realizable secondary sale price exceeds the full cost of creating and delivering units, a participant may create units and sell them. When the realizable redemption proceeds exceed the full cost of buying units, a participant may buy and redeem them.

These activities can bring market price closer to portfolio value. They depend on available inventory, eligibility, fees, dealing windows, funding, and processing capacity. They do not guarantee a fixed price or a risk-free trade.

At a reference NAV of USD 10, a hypothetical USD 0.08 per-unit creation-and-sale cost implies a static breakeven above USD 10.08. A USD 0.07 purchase-and-redemption cost implies a static breakeven below USD 9.93. Price changes while the trade is processed can eliminate either opportunity.

## Trading outside underlying market hours

A pool can remain tradable while the underlying securities market is closed. Liquidity providers then bear inventory and price-discovery risk, and spreads or deviations from the last NAV may increase. A live blockchain does not make underlying assets continuously redeemable.

Use only the official pool addresses and supported routes identified for the product. Independent pools can have different liquidity, fees, and risks.
