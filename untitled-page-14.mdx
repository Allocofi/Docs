## Color Palette

Alloco uses a restrained palette built primarily around black, white, and a distinctive accent green.

### Alloco Navy

`#07192F`

**Alloco Navy is the primary brand color.**

It should be used for high-recognition brand applications such as:

- Hero backgrounds
- Brand covers
- Presentation title pages
- Key product moments
- Institutional communications

White is the preferred logo and typography color when placed on Alloco Navy.

### Alloco Slate

`#4C5960`

Alloco Slate provides a softer secondary tone for:

- Supporting text
- Secondary UI surfaces
- Charts and data visualization
- Borders and dividers
- Secondary brand applications

### Alloco Mist

`#C7CCC6`

Alloco Mist introduces a subtle neutral tone while preserving the restrained visual system.

Recommended uses include:

- Secondary backgrounds
- Cards
- Section separation
- Data visualization
- Editorial layouts

### Black & White

Black and white form the foundation of the Alloco visual system.

They should remain dominant across documentation, product interfaces, reports, and institutional materials.

## Color Principle

The Alloco palette should feel:

**Calm. Institutional. Precise.**

Avoid introducing unnecessary accent colors.

A typical Alloco composition should rely on:

**White / Black → Alloco Navy → Slate / Mist**

Alloco Navy should create recognition, while Slate and Mist should support hierarchy rather than compete for attention.

## Typography

### Primary Typeface

**Inter**

Inter is Alloco's primary typeface across digital products, documentation, presentations, and communications.

<Frame>
  ![1785931439063](/images/1785931439063.jpg "1785931439063")
</Frame>

| Weight | Usage |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Regular 400** | Body copy |
| **Medium 500** | Navigation and labels |
| **SemiBold 600** | Subheadings |
| **Bold 700** | Headlines and key statements |

### Typography Principle

Typography should feel clear, editorial, and deliberate.

Use:

- Generous whitespace
- Short headlines
- Clear hierarchy
- Restrained font weights
- Comfortable line spacing

Avoid excessive bolding, decorative typefaces, or overly dense layouts.

## Logo Usage

### Do

- Use official logo assets
- Maintain the original proportions
- Maintain sufficient clear space
- Use the white logo on Alloco Navy
- Use the black logo on white or light backgrounds
- Preserve strong contrast

### Don't

- Stretch or distort the logo
- Rotate the logo
- Recreate the wordmark
- Change individual logo elements
- Add shadows, gradients, or visual effects
- Place the logo on visually complex backgrounds
- Use unapproved colors for the logo

<Note>
  For partnerships, press, co-branding, or other external applications, please use the official assets provided here rather than recreating or modifying the Alloco identity.
</Note>
