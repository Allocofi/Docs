---
title: "Thematic ETFTs"
---

Thematic ETFTs turn a defined investment idea into asset-selection and weighting rules. A theme becomes a product only when its universe, selection criteria, risk limits, and update policy are specified.

## Theme types

| Type | Investment focus | Illustrative concept |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Industry | Businesses within a defined economic activity | AI infrastructure |
| Structural trend | A change spanning industries or consumer behavior | Digital consumption |
| Attention | Changes in interest, visibility, or engagement measured through defined data | Gen Z attention |

Attention is a subtype of Thematic ETFTs. Not every theme uses social or engagement data.

## The Gen Z example

A Gen Z portfolio can be constructed around businesses associated with a defined consumption or cultural theme. Alternatively, it can use a rules-based signal measuring attention within specified data sources.

These are distinct methodologies. A product using general social data cannot claim to measure the preferences of an entire age group unless the data supports that conclusion. Where age attribution is unavailable, the signal is described as a thematic proxy.

## Attention-based construction

The methodology defines the sources, sample coverage, observation period, signal decay, and connection between the theme and each eligible security. It also defines how automated activity, paid promotion, duplicate content, and missing data are treated.

Liquidity filters, single-name limits, persistence requirements, and turnover controls prevent a raw popularity ranking from becoming an unconstrained trading instruction. A data outage follows the published fallback policy rather than triggering discretionary substitutions.

## Theme changes

A theme's relevance and its investment performance can diverge. Popularity does not guarantee profitability or a favorable valuation.

The product specifies when constituents change, how assets leave the eligible universe, and whether a material change to the theme requires a methodology update or product termination.
