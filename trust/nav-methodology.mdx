GAIN, or Growth Asset Income Notes, is an onchain investment product designed to provide access to an institutional market-neutral digital asset strategy. The underlying strategy seeks returns from funding-rate spreads, cross-market pricing inefficiencies and liquidity provision while maintaining controlled directional exposure.

### How GAIN Works

GAIN provides onchain access to a market-neutral digital-asset strategy through a simple subscription and redemption process.

1. Confirm Regional Availability

**Review the list of restricted jurisdictions before accessing GAIN.** GAIN is not available to users located in, incorporated in, or ordinarily resident in restricted jurisdictions. No separate eligibility verification may be required solely for geographic availability purposes. However, GAIN may conduct identity verification, sanctions screening, wallet screening or other compliance checks where applicable.

2. Connect a Compatible Wallet

Connect a supported self-custodial wallet to the GAIN application. Users remain responsible for maintaining control of their wallets, private keys and transaction permissions.

3. Subscribe Using Supported Stablecoins

Enter the subscription amount and transfer a supported stablecoin. GAIN does not charge a subscription fee, although blockchain network fees may apply.

4. Receive GAIN at the Applicable NAV

Once the subscription transaction is accepted, GAIN units are issued based on the applicable NAV per unit. NAV data uses UTC (UTC\+0) as the reference time zone.

5. Hold or Request Redemption

Users may continue holding GAIN or submit a redemption request through the application.

- **Standard Redemption:** Settlement within T\+5 business days with no redemption fee.
- **Instant Redemption:** Accelerated settlement, subject to available liquidity, with a 1% redemption fee.

Redemption proceeds are calculated using the applicable NAV and may be adjusted for network costs and any other expenses disclosed in the product documentation.
