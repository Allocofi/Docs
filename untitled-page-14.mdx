## Alloco Brand

Alloco's visual identity reflects a simple idea:

**Institutional finance, without the walls.**

Our identity is built around clarity, restraint, and contrast — combining a predominantly black-and-white visual system with a distinctive Alloco green.

> **Prosperity, Unwalled**

## Logo

The Alloco logo is the primary representation of our brand and should remain clear, consistent, and unobstructed across all applications.

### Primary Logo

Use the primary Alloco logo whenever sufficient space is available.

## Color Palette

Alloco uses a restrained palette built primarily around black, white, and a distinctive accent green.

### Primary Colors

| Color | HEX | Usage |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| **Alloco Black** | `#000000` | Primary text, dark backgrounds |
| **Alloco White** | `#FFFFFF` | Light backgrounds, reversed text |
| **Alloco Green** | `#9BFF36` | Primary accent, highlights, key interactions |

### Color Principle

Black and white should remain dominant.

**Alloco Green is an accent — not a background language.**

Use it selectively for:

- Key actions
- Active states
- Data highlights
- Small graphic elements
- Brand moments

Avoid overusing green across large areas or multiple competing elements.

## Typography

### Primary Typeface

**Inter**

Inter is Alloco's primary typeface across digital products, documentation, presentations, and communications.

Recommended weights:

| Weight | Usage |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Regular 400** | Body copy |
| **Medium 500** | Labels and navigation |
| **SemiBold 600** | Subheadings |
| **Bold 700** | Headlines and key statements |

### Typography Principle

Typography should feel:

**Clear. Calm. Precise.**

Use generous spacing, short headlines, and strong hierarchy. Avoid excessive bolding, decorative typefaces, or dense layouts.

## Logo Usage

### Do

- Maintain sufficient clear space around the logo
- Use official logo files
- Maintain the original proportions
- Ensure strong contrast with the background
- Use black or white versions whenever possible

### Don't

- Stretch or distort the logo
- Rotate the logo
- Add shadows, gradients, or effects
- Change individual parts of the logo
- Place the logo over visually complex backgrounds
- Recreate the logo using another typeface

## Brand in Practice

The Alloco visual system should feel closer to an institutional financial publication than a typical crypto interface.

Prioritize:

- Generous whitespace
- Strong typography
- Restrained use of color
- Clear financial information
- Minimal ornamentation
- High-quality editorial imagery

The result should feel **institutional, contemporary, and quietly distinctive**.
