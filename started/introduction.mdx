### **01. Onchain ETFT**

**The simplicity of an ETF, the power of onchain markets**

An Onchain ETF Token (ETFT) brings the portfolio structure and simplicity of an ETF onchain, packaging a diversified basket of assets, systematic strategy, or market theme into a single tokenized product. Powered by smart contracts, each ETFT integrates predefined portfolio rules, programmatic rebalancing, real-time NAV tracking, minting and redemption and continuous liquidity—giving investors a simpler way to access and manage diversified exposure through a single token.

### **02.** ETFT Launcher

**Turn a market view into an investable product**

ETFT Launcher is Alloco’s product infrastructure for transforming investment ideas, portfolios, and strategies into structured Onchain ETFTs.

From product configuration and portfolio rules to NAV, minting, redemption, and lifecycle management, Launcher provides the infrastructure to bring an investment strategy onchain.
