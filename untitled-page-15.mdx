## About This Statement

This Risk Statement provides a general overview of certain material risks associated with acquiring, holding, transferring and redeeming **GAIN (Growth Asset Income Notes)** and using the related services provided by **Allocofi Limited** (“Allocofi”, “we”, “us” or “our”).

GAIN is an onchain product that provides access to a systematic digital-asset strategy seeking returns from market-neutral and relative-value opportunities, including funding-rate spreads, cross-market pricing inefficiencies and liquidity-related opportunities.

This Statement is provided for risk disclosure purposes only. It is not a prospectus, offering memorandum or solicitation document. Nothing in this Statement constitutes investment, legal, tax, accounting or financial advice, or a recommendation that GAIN is suitable for any particular person.

The risks described in this Statement are not exhaustive. Other risks may exist or arise that are not described here.

You should conduct your own assessment of GAIN based on your circumstances, experience, financial resources, objectives and risk tolerance.

You should not acquire, hold or transact in GAIN unless you understand the nature of the product and are able to bear the risk of partial or total loss.

## Regulatory Characterisation

GAIN is **not intended to constitute, and is not offered as, a security, share, equity interest or investment contract**.

GAIN has not been registered under the U.S. Securities Act of 1933, as amended, or under the securities laws of any other jurisdiction.

Holding GAIN does not provide any ownership interest, shareholder rights, governance rights, voting rights, dividend rights or equity participation in Allocofi Limited or any affiliated entity.

Subject to the applicable Product Terms, each GAIN represents a proportional economic interest in the net assets attributable to the GAIN product.

Holding GAIN does not give a holder direct ownership or control of any specific asset, trading position, wallet or institutional trading account used in connection with the GAIN strategy.

A holder does not, solely by holding GAIN, have a direct contractual relationship with or direct claim against any exchange, broker, custodian, technology provider or other service provider used by Allocofi.

The regulatory treatment of GAIN, Allocofi and related activities may differ between jurisdictions and may change over time. Access to GAIN may therefore be restricted based on applicable law, regulatory requirements, sanctions or other compliance considerations.

## How GAIN Value Is Determined

GAIN does not provide a fixed or guaranteed return.

The value of GAIN is determined by the applicable NAV per GAIN, calculated in accordance with the GAIN NAV Methodology.

NAV reflects the net value of assets and liabilities attributable to GAIN and may include:

- stablecoin and cash balances;
- spot digital-asset positions;
- futures and perpetual positions;
- realized and unrealized profit or loss;
- funding income and expense;
- trading, borrowing and financing costs;
- applicable product liabilities; and
- applicable fees.

Changes in NAV therefore reflect the actual net performance of the GAIN strategy and related costs.

Neither Allocofi nor any other person guarantees that NAV will increase, remain stable or return to any previous level.

## 1. Return and Strategy Risk

GAIN seeks returns from market inefficiencies rather than from a directional view on the broader digital-asset market.

Market neutrality is a strategy objective, not a guarantee.

Positions intended to offset each other may become temporarily or materially mismatched because of partial or failed execution, changes in position delta, contract differences, mark-price differences, collateral movements, margin requirements or rapidly changing market conditions.

A portfolio may have low net directional exposure while maintaining substantial gross exposure. Low net exposure should therefore not be interpreted as low overall risk.

The strategy also depends on market data, quantitative analysis, risk parameters and execution processes. These may not perform as expected under all market conditions.

Returns may be positive, zero or negative.

Historical results, annualized rates, strategy track records, examples or illustrative calculations do not guarantee future performance. Any strategy track record that predates GAIN does not represent the actual performance of GAIN.

## 2. Funding and Relative-Value Risk

The strategy may seek returns from differences in funding rates and prices across spot and derivatives markets.

Funding rates may decline, reverse or remain unfavorable for extended periods.

Spot and perpetual prices may not converge as expected, and prices or mark values for similar instruments may diverge across trading venues.

Differences in funding schedules, mark-price methodologies, index construction and settlement rules may affect realized returns.

A position that is intended to be hedged may therefore incur losses even where overall market direction is not the primary source of risk.

## 3. Execution Risk

GAIN strategies may require two or more related transactions to be executed at approximately the same time.

There is no assurance that each transaction can be executed at the expected price, in the expected size, at the same time or at all.

One leg of a trade may execute while another is delayed, partially filled, rejected or cancelled. This may create temporary directional exposure or cause the expected spread to disappear.

Losses may result from:

- slippage;
- market impact;
- insufficient order-book depth;
- exchange latency;
- API or market-data outages;
- order rejection;
- trading suspension; or
- sudden price movements.

Execution controls may reduce these risks but cannot eliminate them.

## 4. Leverage and Margin Risk

GAIN may use derivatives and other positions that create gross exposure greater than the amount of capital posted as margin.

Leverage may improve capital efficiency but can also magnify losses.

Different trading venues may apply different margin requirements, liquidation methodologies, collateral rules and mark prices.

A portfolio that is broadly neutral on an aggregate basis may still experience material margin pressure at an individual trading venue.

If available margin becomes insufficient, positions may need to be reduced or closed under unfavorable conditions or may be forcibly liquidated.

## 5. Trading Venue, Counterparty and Concentration Risk

Assets attributable to GAIN may be deployed through institutional accounts maintained with approved centralized trading venues.

Trading venues and other counterparties may experience:

- insolvency or illiquidity;
- withdrawal suspension;
- cyberattack;
- fraud or misconduct;
- operational failure;
- regulatory action;
- account restrictions; or
- settlement failure.

Assets held with an affected counterparty may become inaccessible or may be partially or completely lost.

At certain times, a material amount of GAIN capital, collateral or gross exposure may also be concentrated at a particular venue or in a particular underlying asset.

Allocofi may diversify exposure and apply venue or concentration controls, but such measures cannot eliminate counterparty or concentration risk.

The strategy may also require capital or collateral to be moved between venues. Transfers may be delayed by withdrawal restrictions, blockchain congestion, security procedures, compliance checks or operational outages, which may affect hedging, margin management or the ability to capture trading opportunities.

## 6. Stablecoin and Collateral Risk

GAIN accepts subscriptions in supported stablecoins, currently including USDT and USDC, and may use stablecoins as strategy collateral or settlement assets.

Stablecoins may deviate from their intended reference value or experience issuer, reserve, banking, liquidity, redemption, operational or regulatory disruption.

A trading venue may also reduce the collateral value assigned to a stablecoin or cease accepting it as collateral.

If a stablecoin used by GAIN loses value or becomes impaired, this may negatively affect NAV, strategy operations or redemption proceeds.

## 7. Liquidity and Redemption Risk

Positions used by GAIN may not always be capable of being closed or converted into available liquidity at the expected price or within the expected period.

Liquidity may deteriorate rapidly during periods of market stress, exchange disruption or unusual volatility. Closing positions quickly may result in increased slippage or market impact.

GAIN currently provides two redemption routes, subject to the Product Terms and Redemption Policy.

**Standard Redemption**

- settlement target of T\+5 business days;
- no standard redemption fee.

**Instant Redemption**

- intended for accelerated settlement;
- subject to immediately available liquidity;
- currently subject to a 1% redemption fee.

These settlement periods are targets and are not unconditional guarantees.

Standard and instant redemptions currently share a product-level daily redemption gate equal to **30% of the previous day's Final AUM**.

Requests exceeding available daily capacity may enter a queue and be processed on subsequent days.

Redemptions may also be delayed by market conditions, exchange restrictions, blockchain congestion, security incidents, compliance requirements, operational issues or other exceptional circumstances.

The availability of instant redemption should not be interpreted as a guarantee that all GAIN holdings can be redeemed immediately.

## 8. NAV and Pricing Risk

GAIN transactions are based on the applicable official NAV in accordance with the Product Terms.

NAV calculations depend on exchange balances, open positions, market prices, funding information and other operational data.

Valuation may be affected by:

- stale or inaccurate market prices;
- incorrect exchange balances;
- delayed position data;
- API or data outages;
- reconciliation differences; or
- exceptional market conditions.

In certain circumstances, NAV publication may be delayed, may require estimation or alternative valuation inputs, or may subsequently be corrected in accordance with the NAV Methodology.

If GAIN is transferred or traded through a secondary venue, its market price may differ from the official NAV.

The official NAV is a valuation reference and is not a guaranteed secondary-market execution price.

## 9. Onchain Technology and Transfer Risk

GAIN operates on supported public blockchain networks and depends on smart contracts and related software.

Blockchain networks may experience congestion, high transaction fees, forks, outages, attacks or other disruptions.

Smart contracts may contain defects or behave unexpectedly. Security reviews and audits may reduce these risks but cannot establish that smart-contract code is free from all vulnerabilities.

Certain smart-contract functions may depend on privileged roles used for activities such as minting, burning, upgrades or emergency actions. Compromise or misuse of privileged permissions may result in disruption or loss.

GAIN may also be transferred between compatible blockchain addresses, subject to the Product Terms and applicable restrictions.

Blockchain transactions are generally irreversible. GAIN sent to an incorrect address, unsupported network or incompatible smart contract may become permanently inaccessible.

Technical transferability does not mean that every recipient is legally eligible to hold GAIN or that every transfer will be recognized for product purposes.

## 10. Technology and Operational Risk

GAIN depends on wallets, exchange systems, APIs, software, servers, network infrastructure, data sources and third-party technology and operational providers.

Losses or disruption may result from:

- wallet or credential compromise;
- API-key leakage;
- unauthorized access;
- software defects;
- cyberattacks;
- incorrect permissions;
- transaction errors;
- reconciliation failures;
- market-data failures;
- infrastructure outages; or
- human or internal-control failures.

A failure by a third-party provider may affect GAIN even where Allocofi's own systems continue to operate normally.

Access controls, multisignature arrangements, monitoring, reconciliation and other operational safeguards may reduce these risks but cannot eliminate them.

## 11. Conflicts of Interest

Allocofi acts as both issuer of GAIN and manager of the underlying strategy.

This may create conflicts in connection with matters such as:

- valuation;
- fee calculation;
- venue selection;
- trade allocation;
- liquidity management;
- acceptance or processing of subscriptions and redemptions; and
- suspension or emergency decisions.

Allocofi may implement procedures intended to identify and manage conflicts, but such procedures cannot eliminate every potential conflict.

## 12. Legal, Compliance and Tax Risk

The laws and regulatory requirements applicable to digital assets, blockchain-based products, derivatives, trading activities and related services continue to evolve.

Changes in law, regulation, regulatory interpretation, sanctions or enforcement may affect:

- access to GAIN;
- subscriptions or redemptions;
- transfers;
- supported jurisdictions;
- trading venues;
- stablecoin use;
- strategy operations; or
- other aspects of the product.

Allocofi may conduct identity verification, sanctions screening, wallet screening, transaction monitoring or other compliance checks where applicable.

A subscription, transfer or redemption may be rejected, delayed or restricted as a result of legal or compliance requirements.

Technical access to the GAIN application or smart contract does not mean that GAIN is lawfully available to every person or in every jurisdiction.

The tax treatment of acquiring, holding, transferring or redeeming GAIN may differ between jurisdictions and may change over time. GAIN transactions may result in tax liabilities, reporting requirements or other obligations.

Allocofi does not provide legal or tax advice.

## 13. Exceptional Events and Product Actions

Multiple risks described in this Statement may occur at the same time.

Examples include:

- failure or withdrawal suspension at a major trading venue;
- severe stablecoin dislocation;
- widespread market liquidations;
- blockchain disruption;
- significant cybersecurity incidents;
- unusual market volatility; or
- legal or regulatory intervention.

During such events, liquidity may deteriorate, hedges may become less effective, margin requirements may increase and redemptions may be delayed.

Subject to the Product Terms, Allocofi may restrict or suspend subscriptions, redemptions, transfers, strategy deployment or other product functions where necessary because of market, liquidity, security, legal, compliance or operational circumstances.

GAIN may also be discontinued or wound down.

A wind-down may require positions to be closed and assets to be recovered from trading venues or other counterparties. This may take longer than expected and may occur under unfavorable market conditions.

Final proceeds may therefore be delayed or lower than an earlier published NAV.

Risk-management measures may reduce the likelihood or impact of certain events but cannot guarantee uninterrupted operations, successful execution, timely redemption or positive performance.

A severe event, or combination of events, may result in substantial or complete loss.

## 14. Limits of Transparency

Allocofi may publish information relating to GAIN NAV, performance, supply, portfolio risk, liquidity and material events.

Onchain records may provide visibility into GAIN issuance, transfers and burns.

Onchain activity does not, however, provide complete visibility into assets, liabilities and positions maintained within institutional trading accounts.

Published risk metrics are point-in-time measurements and may change rapidly.

Transparency should not be interpreted as a guarantee of solvency, liquidity, security or future performance.

Neither principal nor returns are guaranteed.

Market-neutral does not mean risk-free.

Redemptions may be delayed.

Trading venues, counterparties, stablecoins, blockchains, smart contracts and operational systems may fail.
